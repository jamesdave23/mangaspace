Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a series?

In the Library tab, tap + and search for a title. Open it and tap Add to Library. Series information and covers come from MangaDex.

How do I mark volumes as owned or read?

On a series page, tap a cover to mark it read. Use the checkbox under each cover to mark it owned, or touch and hold for more options.

Why can’t I read some chapters?

Chapters come from MangaDex, which doesn’t host every licensed series. When chapters aren’t available, the series page links to official stores where you can buy the volumes. You can also import your own CBZ, PDF, or image files.

How do new chapter alerts and release reminders work?

Turn them on in Profile → Settings → Notifications. MangaSpace checks for new chapters in the background and reminds you before English volumes you don’t own come out. You can turn alerts off for individual series from their page.

Does my library sync between devices?

Yes. When you’re signed in to iCloud, your library, reading progress, and profile name sync automatically through your private iCloud account. Check the status in Profile → Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without an account. Posting reviews, discussions, or comments uses Sign in with Apple, and only the display name you choose is shown to other readers.

How do I report or block someone in the community?

Tap the ⋯ button on any review, discussion, or comment and choose Report or Block. Blocking hides everything that person posts. Manage blocked readers in Profile → Settings → Community.

How do I delete my community account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Community → Delete Community Account. This permanently removes your profile and everything you’ve posted. Your library isn’t affected.
